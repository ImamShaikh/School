About Our Institution

History, Mission & Leadership

Our Legacy & Trust

Founded in 1994, Govind Ashramshala has been a beacon of quality education and holistic development for students in the region.

The school operates under the esteemed guidance of the **Dnyanganga Shikshan Prasarak Mandal**, an educational trust committed to shaping young minds through academic excellence and value-based learning. At the heart of Govind Ashramshala is a team of dedicated leaders and educators who strive to create an environment where every child is encouraged to learn, grow, and succeed.

Distinguished Leadership

The visionaries driving academic structure, infrastructure development, and community support at our institution.

Chairman

Adv. Ram Hari Rupanawar

Ex-MLC | Congressman | Advocate

Mr. Ram Hari Rupanawar is a visionary leader, dedicated public servant, and passionate advocate for education and social welfare. As the Chairman of Govind Ashramshala, he has played a pivotal role in transforming the institution into a hub of quality education, discipline, and holistic development. With a distinguished career in public service, Mr. Rupanawar currently serves as a respected Member of the Legislative Council (MLC). Under his dynamic leadership, Govind Ashramshala continues to grow as a beacon of opportunity for students in rural and tribal areas.

Headmaster / Principal

Mr. Mahamad Kasim Shaikh

Headmaster Academics

Mr. Mahamad Kasim Shaikh is an experienced and dedicated educator who has been the driving force behind the academic excellence and disciplined environment of Govind Ashramshala. With a deep commitment to nurturing young minds, he brings a unique blend of leadership, compassion, and educational expertise to the institution. He leads the academic faculty with integrity, encouraging innovative classroom teaching methods, regular assessments, and robust character development.

Secretary

Mr. Adinath Rupanawar

Ayurvedic Professional | Administrator

Mr. Adinath Rupanawar is a dynamic and dedicated individual who brings a unique combination of healthcare expertise and social commitment to his role as Secretary of Govind Ashramshala. As Secretary, he plays a crucial role in the strategic planning, smooth administration, and financial health of the institution. He advocates for an education system that blends academic learning with cultural values and discipline.
